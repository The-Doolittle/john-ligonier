# johnligonier.com

An archive and memorial site for Ralph Kinney Bennett (March 3, 1941 – December 18, 2024): journalist, longtime writer and editor at *The Reader's Digest*, Ligonier historian, and sometimes "John Ligonier."

The site gathers his articles, essays and columns, his television appearances, his biography and obituary, so family, friends and readers can explore his work at their own pace. Visitors can also send a memory privately to the family.

Maintained by his family.
