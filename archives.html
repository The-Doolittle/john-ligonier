The Written Archives

A collection of books, historical essays, and articles by Ralph Bennett.

Ligonier 250: The Fort – The Town – The Valley (Excerpts)

Published 2008

Commissioned to commemorate the 250th anniversary of Fort Ligonier, this comprehensive volume chronicles the area's rich military origins and its evolution into a vibrant, enduring community.

The Valley's Voice: Memories of the Ligonier Echo

Historical Essay

A personal and historical reflection on the role of local journalism in shaping the community, drawing upon his mother Virginia's time as the editor of the Ligonier Echo during the 1970s.

Selected Reporting: The Reader's Digest Years

1970s – 2000s

An archival collection of Ralph's national journalism, featuring insights from his interviews with prominent political figures and his on-the-ground reporting from Washington, D.C., Europe, and Central America.
